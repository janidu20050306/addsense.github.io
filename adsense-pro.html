// The Process

Four Steps to Your First Paycheck

The proven path from zero to passive income with Google AdSense.

01

Build Your Site

Create a content-rich website in a high-CPC niche. Focus on quality, original content that ranks organically in search results.

02

Apply to AdSense

Submit your site for review. Ensure content meets policy requirements — original, sufficient, and advertiser-friendly.

03

Place Ads Strategically

Use Auto Ads or manually place units for maximum viewability. Above-the-fold and in-content placements perform best.

04

Scale & Optimize

Grow organic traffic, experiment with ad formats, track RPM, and reinvest into content to compound earnings over time.
